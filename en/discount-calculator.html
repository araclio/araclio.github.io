How to Calculate a Discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to find the amount you save.

Discount amount:
Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Subtract the discount amount from the original price to calculate the final sale price.

Final price:
Original Price − Discount Amount
