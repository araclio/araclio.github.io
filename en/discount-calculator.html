How to Calculate a Discount

To calculate a discount, first find the percentage of the original price that will be deducted.

Discount amount formula:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Then subtract the discount amount from the original price.

Final price formula:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

What Is 20% Off 100?

To calculate 20% off 100:

100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 20

The discount amount is 20.

100 − 20 = 80

The final price after a 20% discount is 80.

What Is 25% Off 200?

200 × 25 ÷ 100 = 50

The discount amount is 50.

200 − 50 = 150

The final price is 150.

What Is 30% Off 500?

500 × 30 ÷ 100 = 150

The discount amount is 150.

500 − 150 = 350

The final sale price is 350.

How Much Do I Save?

The amount you save is the discount percentage of the original price.

For example, if a product costs 250 and has a 20% discount:

250 × 20 ÷ 100 = 50

You save 50, and the final price becomes:

250 − 50 = 200

How to Calculate the Sale Price

The sale price is the amount you pay after the discount has been removed from the original price.

Another way to calculate it directly is:

Final Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100)

For example, a 20% discount on 500:

500 × 0.80 = 400
