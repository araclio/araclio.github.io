What Does the Word Counter Measure?

The tool displays the total number of words in your text, the total number of characters and the number of characters excluding whitespace.

Words are separated using whitespace such as spaces and line breaks.

What Can a Word Counter Be Used For?

A word counter can be useful when writing articles, essays, assignments, product descriptions, social media posts, website content and other text with length requirements.

The calculation runs directly in your browser and updates instantly while you type.
