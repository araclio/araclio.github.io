How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Formula:
Result = Number × Percentage ÷ 100

For example, to find 15% of 1,500:

1,500 × 15 ÷ 100 = 225
