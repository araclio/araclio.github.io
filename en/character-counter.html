What Is a Character Counter?

A character counter measures the number of individual characters in a piece of text.

Characters can include:

* Letters
* Numbers
* Spaces
* Punctuation
* Symbols
* Line breaks

Character counting is useful when writing text for platforms, forms or fields that have length limits.

Characters With Spaces vs. Without Spaces

Characters with spaces counts every character entered in the text box, including spaces and punctuation.

Characters without spaces removes whitespace such as spaces, tabs and line breaks before calculating the total.

For example:

Hello world

contains:

11 characters with the space
10 characters without the space

How Are Words Counted?

Words are counted by separating non-empty text using whitespace such as spaces, tabs and line breaks.

For example:

Hello world

contains 2 words.

Sentence Counter

The tool also estimates the number of sentences in your text.

Sentence endings are generally detected using:

. period
! exclamation mark
? question mark

Sentence counting is an estimate and may vary with abbreviations, unusual punctuation or special formatting.

Line Counter

The line counter displays how many separate lines are present in your text.

This can be useful for:

* Lists

* Captions
* Code snippets
* Poetry
* Structured text
