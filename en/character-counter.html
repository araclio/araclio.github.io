What Is a Character Count?

A character count measures the number of characters contained in a piece of text. Characters can include letters, numbers, punctuation marks and spaces.

Character limits are commonly used for forms, descriptions, headlines, messages and online content.

Characters With Spaces vs. Without Spaces

Character count with spaces includes every character entered in the text box, including whitespace.

Character count without spaces removes whitespace before calculating the total. This makes it easy to compare both measurements instantly.
