What Is a Character Count?

A character count measures how many individual characters appear in a piece of text.

Characters may include:

* Letters
* Numbers
* Spaces
* Punctuation marks
* Symbols
* Line breaks

Character limits are commonly used for social media posts, forms, headlines, descriptions, advertisements, applications and online publishing.

Characters With Spaces vs. Without Spaces

Characters with spaces counts every character entered in the text box, including spaces and punctuation.

Characters without spaces removes whitespace such as spaces, tabs and line breaks before calculating the total.

Because different platforms may define character limits differently, seeing both totals can be useful.

How Are Words Counted?

The word counter separates text using spaces, tabs and line breaks.

For example:

Hello world

contains:

11 characters including the space
10 characters without the space
2 words

Sentence Counter
