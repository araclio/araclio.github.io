How Is Markup Calculated?

Markup is the amount added to the cost of a product to determine its selling price. This calculator applies the percentage you enter to the original cost.

Profit amount:
Cost × Markup Percentage ÷ 100

Selling price:
Cost + Profit Amount

For example, if a product costs 500 and you apply a 30% markup, the profit amount is 150 and the selling price is 650.

Markup Calculation Examples

Profit: 150
Selling price: 650

Cost 1,000 + 20% markup:
Profit: 200
Selling price: 1,200

Cost 2,000 + 25% markup:
Profit: 500
Selling price: 2,500

Markup vs. Profit Margin

Markup and profit margin are related, but they are not the same calculation. Markup is calculated as a percentage of cost, while profit margin is generally calculated as a percentage of the selling price.

This calculator uses markup based on cost.
